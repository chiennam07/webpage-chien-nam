Welcome, welcome!

I hadn't thought much to write. For now, delight yourselves in the paragraphs I have woven!

I appreciate that you took the time to come visit my webpage and see it through. Of course I can't just tell you all MY life story. I have instead opted to share some current day aspects. Against my parents' better judgment, I am still gaming despite it being my second year of university. No, it is NOT my whole life! With some better judgment, my time gaming would rather be, when either I have finished most or all work, or I got plenty of time (Note: Time given before a big exam does not count!).

You'd be surprised how most of my day would go. I actually binge-watch YouTube videos. And when I mean binge-watch, I mean it to the max! A creator with eye-catching thumbnails? I'd zip through their catalog in a week. An unknown creator pops up on my radar?? Done! You get the gist. Rinse and repeat, until I'd inevitably return to the same old videos. It's sad in a way. As a sort of remedy, I… do other stuff!

Like, heading to the park for a morning walk on the weekends, or… exercising at home. I would fail a lot, since I couldn't maintain some stances for long duration. But I always get back up and try again. Simple and neat, wouldn't you say?

Wait a moment… I've forgotten an aspect most intriguing! Some may find this off-putting, but I... would pace around... talking to myself of all people. And stuff I would ponder could range from which font style best be used to if I should leg away to yonder for no reason. Even as mundane as my life is, I just find ways to make things either difficult or interesting. But even the difficult ones are interesting to me. So, it should be better if I were to say that my actions have "interesting" results.

That's all for this section. Feel free to look around!
